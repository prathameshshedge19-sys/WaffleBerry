# 🧇 Waffle Berry

> **We make people live forever.**

Waffle Berry is an AI-powered memory companion that helps preserve conversations, stories, personalities, and memories of loved ones so they can continue to inspire future generations.

---

# 🌟 Vision

Everyone leaves behind stories, wisdom, and memories.

Waffle Berry aims to preserve those memories through artificial intelligence, allowing future generations to interact with the personality, experiences, and stories of the people they love.

Our long-term goal is to create digital companions that feel natural, personal, and emotionally meaningful.
